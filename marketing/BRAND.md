# ZecPaid Artwork

Approved identity: the golden receipt courier, not the earlier monograms.
Palette: charcoal #101211, yellow #F4B728, pale yellow #FFE099, off-white.

## Exports

- `public/brand/zecpaid-avatar.png`: 1024 x 1024, circular-crop-safe composition.
- `public/brand/zecpaid-banner-x.png`: 1500 x 500.
- `public/brand/zecpaid-mascot-transparent.png`: 1254 x 1254 with alpha.
- Original generated files remain in `brand-sources`.

Mode: built-in image generation. The cutout was generated on chroma green and
processed with the installed imagegen chroma-key helper. Technical resizing
produced the fixed-size exports. Alpha inspection found no opaque green pixels.

## Final Prompt Set

### Character

Design an original character mascot for ZecPaid, a project that pays builders
from token creator fees. A living golden payment receipt, a squat charming
courier with a distinctive asymmetrical folded-paper silhouette: wide golden
paper head/body in one piece, uneven zigzag torn crest, a folded top-right
corner, expressive ink-black square eyes, subtly raised eyebrow and a confident
crooked smile. Black mitten hands and chunky black boots. A black crossbody
strap, golden envelope satchel and a sealed envelope in one hand. Helpful,
cheeky and competent. Premium indie-game pixel art with deliberate chunky
visible pixels. Limited gold, warm yellow, charcoal and off-white palette.
Single full-body character in three-quarter standing pose, centered with wide
padding on charcoal. No coin, shield, hooded hacker, robot, pill, monogram,
lettering, watermark, scenery or soft glow.

### Avatar

Preserve the approved receipt courier's identity, pixel-art design, palette,
face, folded corner, crest, gloves, boots, strap and envelopes. Fit the entire
character inside a centered circle occupying 76% of a square canvas, with
generous charcoal margin. No actual circular badge, ring, words, extra icons,
scenery or crop. Crisp avatar-ready character illustration.

### Banner

Wide 3:1 profile banner, preserving the approved receipt courier. Full body on
the right, holding its envelope outward, not cropped. Charcoal background,
yellow and off-white pixel lettering. Exact text: ZecPaid. Smaller line: Pay the
builders. Generous breathing room and an empty lower-left area for the profile
photo overlap. A restrained pixel message trail between text and character.
No metrics, coin, other logos, busy scenery, glowing orbs or fake 3D.

### Cutout

Preserve the exact character on a perfectly flat solid #00FF00 chroma-key
background. Entire body visible with generous margins. Preserve the folded
paper head, uneven crest, eyes, eyebrow, smile, envelope, strap, satchel, gloves
and boots. No green in the character; no shadows, gradients, texture, floor,
reflections, translucent edges, words, extra objects or watermark.
