## 2. Create a Coin for a Builder

Choose their @. Check the profile. Add your coin's name, ticker and image.

Review the ZEC pair and fee recipient before approving the launch. The
beneficiary gets the creator-fee share. Protocol and LP fees are separate.

A community coin isn't an endorsement from its beneficiary.

## 3. Receive Your ZEC

Connect X and open My ZEC.

Review a fee collection, then choose the amount and a separate Solana receiving
wallet for your claim. Check the provider fee and net ZEC before approving.

Privacy Cash handles the private-withdrawal route. This is ZEC on Solana,
not native shielded Zcash.
